Dot Detection

A two-interval forced-choice demonstration. One interval contains a white dot; the other is blank. Choose the interval in which you saw the dot.

264 pixels/inch is a common value for 11-inch iPad Pro displays; verify for your model. Browser zoom should be 100%. A physical ruler check is recommended.

Dot diameters: 1, 2, 3, 5, and 8 arcmin. Each is presented for 300 ms, with a 600-ms blank between intervals. No trial-by-trial feedback.

The white bar should measure 10 mm with a physical ruler. Adjust the pixels/inch setting if necessary.

For curiosity/research only. This is not a diagnostic test and does not replace ophthalmic assessment. No eye tracking: successful detection does not prove foveal mediation.
